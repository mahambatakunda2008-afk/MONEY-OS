# Shadecode Money

## Product identity

**Shadecode Money** is Shadecode's global money platform for holding, moving, converting, and spending money across currencies and payment rails.

**Core idea:** Money should follow the person, not the country.

**Primary tagline:** Your money. Everywhere.

## Product surface

- Wallet
- Hold
- Send
- Receive
- Exchange
- Pay
- Travel
- Merchant
- Business
- Connect / payment rails

## Naming rule

The customer-facing product name is **Shadecode Money**. `MONEY-OS` is retained only as the repository name temporarily for continuity while the repository and infrastructure are migrated.
